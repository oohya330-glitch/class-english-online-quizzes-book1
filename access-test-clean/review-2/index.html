文法

文法 1
We are not worried about the situation because it has changed _____ the better.
(A) as(B) for(C) to(D) toward
答案：B — for
判斷重點
change for the better＝變得更好。
關鍵搭配／文法
for the better 為固定片語。
文法 2
I have to decide what I will study in college, but this decision is not easy _____.
(A) make(B) making(C) to make(D) in making
答案：C — to make
判斷重點
不定詞可作副詞修飾前面的形容詞 easy。
關鍵搭配／文法
S + be + adj. + to VR。
文法 3
After all the preparation and fierce competition, the participants were eager _____ the result and know who had won first prize.
(A) to see(B) seeing(C) to seeing(D) to have seen
答案：A — to see
判斷重點
eager 後接 to + 原形動詞。
關鍵搭配／文法
be eager to + VR＝渴望……。
文法 4
The rewards for these changes can be big or small; the important thing is that the changes promote positive behaviors and help to make people _____.
(A) smile(B) smiles(C) smiled(D) smiling
答案：A — smile
判斷重點
make 為使役動詞，受詞 people 後接原形動詞。
關鍵搭配／文法
make + O + VR。
文法 5
The long hike had really made the climbers _____; they needed to find a place to rest and refresh themselves.
(A) tired(B) tiring(C) tiredness(D) to tire
答案：A — tired
判斷重點
make + O + OC；tired 為形容詞，用來修飾 climbers。
關鍵搭配／文法
make + O + adj.。
文法 6
Edward’s boss always _____ him do everything in the office.
(A) asks(B) chooses(C) makes(D) wants
答案：C — makes
判斷重點
後方為 him + do，只有使役動詞 make 接 O + VR。
關鍵搭配／文法
make + O + VR；ask/get/want + O + to VR。
文法 7
The coach _____ the basketball players pass the ball and work together as a team.
(A) asked(B) got(C) made(D) wanted
答案：C — made
判斷重點
後方 pass、work 為原形動詞，因此用 made。
關鍵搭配／文法
make + O + VR。
文法 8
If you want to experience living in a large metropolis, why not try moving to New York City _____ is also known as “The Big Apple.”
(A) which(B) , which(C) that(D) , what
答案：B — , which
判斷重點
New York City 是獨一無二的專有名詞，使用非限定關係子句，前面要有逗點。
關鍵搭配／文法
非限定關係子句可用 which，不可用 that。
文法 9
When my sister goes to college, she will study art _____ she has been interested in since she was a little girl.
(A) because(B) what(C) , which(D) , that
答案：C — , which
判斷重點
先行詞 art 為聽者已知的事物，補充說明用非限定關係子句。
關鍵搭配／文法
逗點 + which；非限定用法不可用 that。
文法 10
The science teacher demonstrated how to carry out the experiment _____ that all the students clearly understood the procedures involved.

關鍵搭配／文法
