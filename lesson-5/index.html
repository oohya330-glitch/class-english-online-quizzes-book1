文法 1
If more of our waste _____, we can use fewer natural resources and greatly reduce pollution.
(A) recycle
(B) recycled
(C) is recycling
(D) is recycled
答案：D — is recycled
完整英文句子／答案

中文翻譯／意思

判斷重點
主詞 waste 是「被回收」，需用被動語態 be + p.p.；條件句用現在式 is recycled。
文法 2
Plastic was invented because scientists hoped this new material would _____ items that once had been expensive become affordable to more people.
(A) allow
(B) make
(C) cause
(D) produce
答案：B — make
完整英文句子／答案
Plastic was invented because scientists hoped this new material would make items that once had been expensive become affordable to more people.

判斷重點
make + O + VR：make items become...；allow/cause 需接 O + to VR。

(B) obviously
(C) where
(D) harm

篇章結構

Hello! My name is Milu. I’m a beagle with a white and tan coat. When I was only a puppy, Mom picked me up from the pet shop and took me to her home. __1__ When Mom wasn’t at home, Calvin and I always played my favorite game, “Catch.” Calvin would throw a ball across the living room, and I would catch it and give the ball back to him. We would do this over and over again until Mom came back. At that time, “fun” was the only experience I knew.

__2__ One day, he told me that he didn’t like his new school, his teacher, his friends, or even Mom! He was very sad, and I didn’t know what I could do to help him, so I just lay on the bed beside him. Later, he burst into tears and held me in his arms. He started to talk to me about things that I didn’t understand.

A few years later, Mom decided to move to a new house. This new house was much smaller than our old one. __3__ I also heard Mom saying something about “cutting down on extra costs.” That night, Calvin allowed me to stay inside his bedroom for the whole night. He kept saying “sorry” to me. I fell asleep in his arms, and it felt like the good old days. That was the very last time that I ever saw Calvin. __4__ I don’t think Dusty knows what he is talking about. Calvin would never abandon me; I think he just forgot to take me back home. He is going to pick me up very soon, right?

(A) According to my street friend Dusty, I have been abandoned.
(B) As time went by, little Calvin started to look not so little anymore.
(C) That’s when I met little Calvin, who would become my best friend.
(D) Calvin and I always ate dinner together in the kitchen after Mom came home.
(E) Mom told Calvin that the rent was too high for us to stay in the old house, so we had to move.
答案：1.C, 2.B, 3.E, 4.A
第 1 題：C
That’s when I met little Calvin, who would become my best friend.
中文：那就是我遇見小 Calvin 的時候；他後來成了我最好的朋友。
前句說媽媽把幼犬 Milu 帶回家，後句馬上接 Calvin 與 Milu 的互動，因此 C 最自然介紹 Calvin 的出場。
第 2 題：B
As time went by, little Calvin started to look not so little anymore.
中文：隨著時間過去，小 Calvin 看起來也不再那麼小了。
第一段寫幼年玩耍；下一句開始談新學校與煩惱，B 用時間推移銜接 Calvin 長大。
第 3 題：E
Mom told Calvin that the rent was too high for us to stay in the old house, so we had to move.
中文：媽媽告訴 Calvin，舊房子的房租太高，無法繼續住，所以我們必須搬家。
前文說新房子更小，後文又提到「削減額外支出」，E 說明搬家的經濟原因，銜接最完整。
第 4 題：A
According to my street friend Dusty, I have been abandoned.
中文：根據我的街頭朋友 Dusty 的說法，我已經被遺棄了。
後句立刻回應「I don’t think Dusty knows what he is talking about.」，因此前句必須先提到 Dusty 的說法。
未使用選項：D
Calvin and I always ate dinner together in the kitchen after Mom came home.
中文：媽媽回家後，Calvin 和我總是在廚房一起吃晚餐。
文章各空格的前後文沒有需要補充「一起吃晚餐」的銜接，因此此選項不使用。

In 2017, the United Nations Environment Programme launched its “#CleanSeas” campaign at the World Ocean Summit. The following activity can help us to find out how much garbage we have on our lovely beaches. Here is how it’s done.

1 Go to a sandy ocean beach. Using a stick or simply your finger, draw a circle on the sand. It should be large enough to place all your garbage within it. (A circle with a radius of at least one meter is recommended.)

3 Collect, sort, and count: Collect the garbage you can find nearest to you on the beach. Sort the garbage. Count the number of different pieces of garbage in each garbage category.

4 Calculate the proportions and you will have your own waste pie chart!
