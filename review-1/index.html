文法

文法 1
No one knows _____ the concert by that superstar will be held, so they can’t book the tickets in advance.
(A) which
(B) when
(C) what
(D) why
答案：B — when
文法重點
wh-疑問詞引導的間接問句
完整句子
No one knows when the concert by that superstar will be held, so they can’t book the tickets in advance.
中文翻譯
沒有人知道那位巨星的演唱會何時舉行，所以他們無法事先訂票。
為什麼選這個答案
know 後面可接 wh-疑問詞引導的名詞子句。此處語意是「沒有人知道演唱會何時舉行」，因此用 when。間接問句採「wh-疑問詞＋主詞＋動詞」的陳述句語序。
補充／選項辨析
which 表「哪一個」；what 表「什麼」；why 表「為什麼」，都不符合此處詢問時間的語意。
文法 2
Jessie and her brother argued about _____ should tidy the living room. Jessie said she was too tired to clean up, but her brother insisted it was her turn.
(A) what
(B) who
(C) when
(D) how
答案：B — who
文法重點
wh-疑問詞引導的間接問句
完整句子
Jessie and her brother argued about who should tidy the living room. Jessie said she was too tired to clean up, but her brother insisted it was her turn.

補充／選項辨析
too ... to ... 表「太……以致無法……」；此句中的 it was her turn 表「輪到她了」。

I did not know when my purse _____. I did not notice it until I wanted to pay the bill.

(B) stole
(C) was stealing
(D) was stolen
答案：D — was stolen

完整句子

Four Stages of Culture Shock

There are four stages of culture shock: honeymoon, anxiety, adjustment, and acceptance. The first stage comes with your excitement and positive feelings when you first encounter a new and different culture. The second stage may come after about three months, when you begin to really experience differences between your own culture and this new one. When people cannot get used to a new culture, their feelings of loneliness and homesickness may lead to anxiety. Then the adjustment phase begins after around six to twelve months. At this time, one may begin to develop new routines as he or she becomes more comfortable in the new culture. The last stage, acceptance, still does not mean one has become fully “local,” and now the traveler may tend to become a rejector, an adopter, or a cosmopolitan.

Rejectors are people who cannot accept new cultures. They think that returning to their original culture is the only way out. About 10% of international travelers are adopters. They are just the opposite of the rejectors. Instead of keeping their original culture, they take on the new one. About 30% of international travelers become cosmopolitans. As they develop a mix of their original culture and the new one, they are the ones who are best at living abroad.
