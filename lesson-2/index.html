文法 1
_____ is not easy to master a foreign language.
(A) It
(B) This
(C) That
(D) What
答案：A — It
文法解說：It 為虛主詞，真正主詞是後面的 to master a foreign language。
完整英文句子／答案
It is not easy to master a foreign language.
中文翻譯／意思
精通一門外語並不容易。
判斷重點
It 為虛主詞，真正主詞是後面的 to master a foreign language。
文法 2
_____ is not easy for parents to know what their children are thinking about if they don’t communicate with them.
(A) It
(B) This
(C) That
(D) What
答案：A — It
文法解說：It is + adj. + for sb. + to V 為虛主詞句型。
完整英文句子／答案
It is not easy for parents to know what their children are thinking about if they don’t communicate with them.
中文翻譯／意思
如果父母不和孩子溝通，就不容易知道孩子在想什麼。
判斷重點
It is + adj. + for sb. + to V 為虛主詞句型。
文法 3

文法 9
選文法錯誤的。
(A) If it doesn’t stop to rain tomorrow, it will have been rained for a week.
(B) When I finally managed to get to the train station last night, the train had already left.
(C) There has been something wrong with the washing machine. Right now, it is being repaired by my father.
(D) If the project is to be put into practice, some changes must be made.
答案：A — If it doesn’t stop to rain tomorrow, it will have been rained for a week.
文法解說：stop raining 才是「停止下雨」；rain 為不及物動詞，不能用被動 been rained。
完整英文句子／答案
If it doesn’t stop raining tomorrow, it will have been raining for a week.
中文翻譯／意思

When you want to say goodbye to your girlfriend or boyfriend, you need to write him or her a “Dear John letter.” Here are some tips on how to write a good one.

1 Be clear that you want to stop the relationship. Leave no uncertainty so that your partner will be likely to stop trying.

2 Do not say any negative things. He or she might still stay around for another semester, or even another year, so make it your problem, not his or hers.

3 Keep the letter short and clear. Romances are better when they last longer, while breakups are the opposite.

4 Before sending out your letter, keep it for twenty-four hours. Read it again. Make sure that nothing clearly looks wrong, and then hit the send button. You might want a second pair of eyes to see it before doing so.

5 Thank him or her and remind him or her that the breakup does not mean that he or she is not good enough. Some good reasons might include your needing more personal space or other personal issues.

6 Emails work better than letters. If you do write a letter, you might not want to give it to him or her in person.
