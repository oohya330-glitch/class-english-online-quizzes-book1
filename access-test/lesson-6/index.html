We all know of the Eiffel Tower in Paris. However, did you know that one man sold the Eiffel Tower twice? Victor Lustig was a highly skilled liar from Austria-Hungary. __1__ He is regarded as one of the most notorious liars of his time.

In 1925, Victor Lustig saw that the Eiffel Tower needed repairs and decided to use this as an opportunity to make a fortune. __2__ He told these dealers that the government planned to tear down the tower and sell the resulting scrap metal to them. Lustig asked the six dealers to send their bids to him, and the highest bidder would get the metal from the tower. To make himself more believable, Lustig also arranged a private meeting with one of the dealers. __3__ To return the favor, he would help the dealer win the auction. Without a second thought, the dealer agreed. However, once Lustig had received his bribe money and the money for the tower, he fled to Austria right away.

After a year had passed, Lustig returned to Paris to perform the same trick again. However, unlike the previous time, the police were informed this time, and Lustig was almost caught. However, he still managed to flee to the United States. __4__ He was sentenced to 15 years in prison and eventually passed away on March 9, 1947. Victor Lustig will forever be remembered as the man who sold the Eiffel Tower twice.

(A) Victor Lustig was well known for his deep love of architecture and European landmarks.
(B) He disguised himself as an important government official and invited six scrap metal dealers to a secret meeting.
(C) In the US, he continued on his criminal path but was eventually arrested by the FBI on May 10, 1935.
(D) During the early twentieth century, he committed numerous scams and instances of fraud across Europe and the United States.
(E) He convinced that dealer that he was a corrupt official who was hoping the dealer would give him some bribe money.
答案：1. D　2. B　3. E　4. C
第 1 題｜答案 D
首段介紹 Victor Lustig 是惡名昭彰的騙子，接著應補充他在二十世紀初於歐洲及美國犯下多起詐騙，使上下文銜接自然。
第 2 題｜答案 B
前句提到他看到艾菲爾鐵塔需要修繕並決定藉此詐財；接著應描述他假扮政府官員並召集六名廢料經銷商。
第 3 題｜答案 E
前句說他私下約見其中一名經銷商，後句又說「作為回報，他會幫助經銷商贏得競標」，所以此處應是他暗示自己是貪腐官員、期待對方行賄。
第 4 題｜答案 C
