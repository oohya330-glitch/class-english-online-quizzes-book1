文法

文法 1
I turned to my teacher for advice on interpersonal relationships. My problem was _____ I could refuse others nicely without hurting their feelings.
(A) that(B) how(C) what(D) ×
答案：B — how
完整英文句子／答案
B — how
中文翻譯／意思
我的問題是如何在不傷害別人感受的情況下婉轉拒絕別人。
判斷重點
空格後為完整子句，how 表「如何」，作名詞子句主詞補語。
關鍵搭配／文法
how + S + V 可形成名詞子句。
文法 2
As soon as I stepped off the small plane, I eagerly anticipated the adventure _____ awaited me in Kinmen.
(A) what(B) who(C) that(D) ×
答案：C — that
完整英文句子／答案
C — that
中文翻譯／意思
一下小飛機，我就期待著在金門等著我的冒險。
判斷重點
先行詞 adventure 為事物，關係代名詞在子句中作主詞，不可省略。
關鍵搭配／文法
that / which + V 修飾事物先行詞。
文法 3
As I watched, I suddenly realized _____ to give my husband for Christmas.

中文翻譯／意思

判斷重點
give 後缺直接受詞，用 what；疑問詞 + to VR 可作名詞片語。
關鍵搭配／文法
what to give＝要送什麼。

篇章結構

In the busy city of New Orleans, a young boy named Alex grew up in a neighborhood plagued by poverty and crime. His mother, Elena, worked two jobs to make ends meet and dreamed of a better future for her son. __1__

His music teacher, Mr. Thompson, saw potential in Alex and encouraged him to use music as a way to rise above his situation. __2__

One day, Mr. Thompson introduced Alex to the story of Wynton Marsalis, the famous jazz trumpeter who also grew up in New Orleans. __3__ He practiced very hard, and his grades improved as he applied the discipline from his music studies to his schoolwork.

Years later, Alex graduated with honors and earned a scholarship to a well-known music college. __4__ Their belief in the power of education and music had transformed his life, proving that with passion and guidance, dreams can become reality.

(A) Alex sometimes skipped class to practice music alone in the school auditorium.

(B) Inspired by Marsalis’s journey from a similar background to global acclaim, Alex began to have faith in his own potential.

(C) However, despite the difficulties, Alex found comfort in his school’s music program, where he learned to play the trumpet.

(D) Standing on stage at his graduation, trumpet in hand, Alex dedicated his performance to his mother and Mr. Thompson.

(E) Mr. Thompson often stayed after school to give Alex extra lessons, combining musical instruction with life lessons.
答案：1.C　2.E　3.B　4.D
第 1 題｜答案 C
第一段前文交代 Alex 成長於貧困與犯罪環境，C 以 However, despite the difficulties 承接逆境，並引出音樂成為慰藉。
第 2 題｜答案 E
本段焦點是 Mr. Thompson。E 延續老師對 Alex 的幫助，且後文的 life lessons 能與選項中的內容呼應。
第 3 題｜答案 B
前句介紹同樣在 New Orleans 成長的 Wynton Marsalis；B 的 from a similar background 與前句緊密銜接，並合理引出 Alex 更努力練習。
