文法

文法 1
_____ a YouTuber who regularly shares his travel experiences with his viewers, Jackson spends much of his time editing his films.
(A) As(B) For(C) To(D) With
答案：A
完整句子中文翻譯
身為一名經常與觀眾分享旅遊經驗的 YouTuber，Jackson 花很多時間剪輯影片。
判斷重點／文法解析
As 後接身分，表示「身為／作為……」。For、To、With 都不能表示此處的身分關係。
文法 2
_____ most boys entering their teens, Richard is beginning to be attracted to girls in his class.
(A) As(B) Like(C) As if(D) Likewise
答案：B
完整句子中文翻譯
像大多數進入青少年時期的男孩一樣，Richard 開始被班上的女生吸引。
判斷重點／文法解析
Like 在此為介系詞，表示「像……一樣」，後接名詞片語 most boys。As 常接身分或子句，Likewise 為副詞。
文法 3
To get everything ready for the housewarming party, Mr. and Mrs. Lin spent several hours _____ their house and the backyard.
(A) decorated(B) decorating(C) to decorate(D) for decorating
答案：B
完整句子中文翻譯
為了替喬遷派對做好一切準備，林先生和林太太花了好幾個小時布置房子和後院。
判斷重點／文法解析
spend + 時間 + V-ing 表示「花時間做某事」，因此用 decorating。
文法 4
_____ most students who have just gotten their first smartphones, Jack immediately created several social media accounts.
(A) Like(B) Unlike(C) Similar(D) Different
答案：A
完整句子中文翻譯
像大多數剛拿到第一支智慧型手機的學生一樣，Jack 立刻建立了好幾個社群媒體帳號。
判斷重點／文法解析
Like + 名詞片語 =「像……一樣」。Unlike 表示「不像」，與句意不合；similar、different 不能直接以此結構接名詞片語。

Kyle’s family and job were great. However, things _____ a turn for the worse when he got addicted to gambling.
(A) got(B) had(C) made(D) took

判斷重點／文法解析
take a turn for the worse 是固定片語，表示「情況轉壞」。句子敘述過去，因此用 took。

_____ was in 1998 _____ things took a turn when Chi’s photographs attracted the attention of The Earth Geographic Monthly, which put his photos in their articles.

For any athlete wanting a pair of sports shoes, Nike has been one of the world’s go-to brands since its beginning. __1__ The company has already impressed the world with its high-end products, and it now continues to amaze the public with its love and passion for sports.

It all started when two “nobodies” from Oregon decided to try to sell Japanese shoes in the USA in 1964. At that time, many people around the world were crazy about German shoe brands such as Adidas and Puma. __2__ They introduced a Japanese shoe brand called Onitsuka Tiger to the US, and this brand went viral among runners because of its high quality and comfort. Nike’s founding fathers therefore obtained their seed money for a bigger plan, and thus the company was born.

Over half a century later, Nike is now the world’s most valuable sports brand. __3__ Take basketball as an example; Nike foresaw the potential of Michael Jordan and LeBron James and therefore signed them. Furthermore, the company created a strong connection between itself and such athletes’ fans. Finally, Nike outsources all of its production overseas to keep its manufacturing costs as low as possible while focusing on design, research, and development in the US to maintain the most efficient labor cost structure possible.

Nike’s commercial slogan reads, “Greatness is earned, never awarded.” Some may argue that Nike just happens to do the right thing at the right time. __4__ As a pioneer in the sporting world, Nike continues to live by its motto, “Go hard or go home.”
(A) Many small companies fail because they do not pay attention to fashion trends.
(B) Nike provides athletes with not only sporting gear but also a certain lifestyle and mindset.
(C) However, Bill Bowerman and Phil Knight, the cofounders of Nike, were looking to the other side of the earth.
(D) However, we can’t ignore the continual efforts, unlimited ideas of innovations, and exceptional vision of the company.
(E) Moreover, Nike has a substantial level of brand recognition around the world because of its status among celebrities and professional sportspeople.
答案：1.B　2.C　3.E　4.D
第 1 題｜答案 B
前文引用 Nike 的理念「If you have a body, you are an athlete」，後文接公司產品與運動精神，因此選 B，說明 Nike 不只提供運動用品，也提供一種生活型態與心態。
第 2 題｜答案 C
前文提到 1960 年代大家偏愛德國運動品牌，後文卻說兩位創辦人把日本品牌引進美國；C 的 However 與「looking to the other side of the earth」正好形成轉折並銜接後文。
第 3 題｜答案 E
前句說 Nike 五十多年後成為全球最有價值的運動品牌；後文舉 Michael Jordan、LeBron James 等例子，E 提到 Nike 與名人及職業運動員結合而有高度品牌辨識度，最能銜接。
第 4 題｜答案 D
前句提到有人認為 Nike 只是總在對的時間做對的事；後句以轉折語氣強調其先驅地位。D 的 However 進一步指出不能忽視 Nike 持續的努力、創新與願景，語意最連貫。
