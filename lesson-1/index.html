文法

文法 1
That traveler with a map in his hand _____ lost. Let’s try to help him find his way.
(A) looks
(B) looks like
(C) is looked
(D) looking
答案：A

感官連綴動詞 look 後接形容詞。

完整英文句子
That traveler with a map in his hand looks lost. Let’s try to help him find his way.
中文翻譯
那位手上拿著地圖的旅客看起來迷路了。我們來幫他找路吧。
判斷重點
look 為連綴動詞，後面接形容詞描述主詞狀態，因此用 lost。
高中應注意單字
traveler n. 旅客
文法 2
Without any sugar in it, coffee would _____ a little bitter.
(A) taste
(B) be tasted
(C) taste like
(D) be tasted like
答案：A

taste 作感官連綴動詞，後接形容詞 bitter。

完整英文句子
Without any sugar in it, coffee would taste a little bitter.
中文翻譯
如果不加糖，咖啡喝起來會有點苦。
判斷重點
taste 作連綴動詞表示「嚐起來」，後面接形容詞 bitter。
高中應注意單字
bitter adj. 苦的
文法 3
The boy tasted the green orange _____ and found that it tasted _____.
(A) curious; sour
(B) curiously; sourly
(C) curiously; sour
(D) curious; sourly
答案：C

第一格修飾動作 tasted 用副詞；第二格為連綴動詞後接形容詞。

完整英文句子
The boy tasted the green orange curiously and found that it tasted sour.
中文翻譯
