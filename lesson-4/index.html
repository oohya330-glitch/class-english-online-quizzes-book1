文法 1
Some might think that the training process is very long and difficult. Well, it’s actually far _____ than you might think.
(A) more difficult
(B) easier
(C) less interesting
(D) more dangerous
答案：B — easier
完整英文句子／答案
Some might think that the training process is very long and difficult. Well, it’s actually far easier than you might think.
中文翻譯／意思

判斷重點

判斷重點

文法 6

(A) The woman who had a conversation in the bookstore is my sister.
(B) The gift I got from my mother was a new smartphone.
(C) The building which stands over there is a department store.
(D) There is nothing who I can do about it.
答案：D — There is nothing who I can do about it.
完整英文句子／答案
(D) There is nothing who I can do about it.
中文翻譯／意思

判斷重點
nothing 指事物，不能用 who 引導修飾它的關係子句；應使用 that，例如 There is nothing that I can do about it.
