篇章結構

For a modern human being, money is one of life’s top priorities; everything we do is somehow related to money. However, we rarely pay attention to the traps of money. Companies use psychological strategies to make us buy more things than we actually need. __1__ First, we need to recognize the tricks that companies use on us.

An American economist named Richard Thaler proposed a concept called mental accounting. Basically, this refers to people unconsciously categorizing their money into different mental accounts and thus developing multiple ways of spending money.

Companies also pay attention to how people “pay.” Although a product you have bought may make you feel satisfied, the process of paying for it can still hurt. Companies know this better than you do, so they try their best to reduce the pain of paying. __2__ There are two common strategies behind this idea. The first one is called prepayment. This is where you pay for a product early, such as over the phone or by using your EasyCard. __3__ Credit cards are one of the most common post-payment tools. You receive your product or service first, and then you are charged afterwards. These two methods greatly reduce the pain of paying and thus increase sales.

There are many other strategies that businessmen apply to sell their products, so how can we be smarter consumers? __4__ This may not make much difference in this ever-changing world, but at least you are trying to be a smarter consumer rather than a puppet on a string.

(B) So, how can we spend our hard-earned cash more smartly?
(C) Some people prefer shopping online because it feels more convenient and less stressful.

According to the data provided, which is correct?

判斷重點

素養閱讀 2
Please calculate the total sales volume for drinks from 2013 to 2017.
(A) 317.3 billion.(B) 420.6 billion.(C) 556.3 billion.(D) 1486.4 billion.
答案：C — 556.3 billion.

556.3 billion.
判斷重點

(A) 14 billion.(B) 15 billion.(C) 16 billion.(D) 17 billion.
