文法

文法 1
Have you _____ wondered why there are seven days in a week?
(A) before (B) ever (C) once (D) much
答案：B — ever
文法重點
現在完成式與 ever
完整英文句子
Have you ever wondered why there are seven days in a week?
中文翻譯
你曾經想過為什麼一星期有七天嗎？
為什麼選這個答案
句型 Have you ever + p.p.? 用來詢問『是否曾經有過某種經驗』。wondered 是 wonder 的過去分詞，因此搭配 ever 最自然。
選項辨析／補充
ever『曾經』常見於現在完成式疑問句；before『以前』通常放在句尾；once『曾經／一次』不放在 have 與 p.p. 之間表此義；much 不能修飾 wondered。
文法 2
Can you tell me _____?
(A) which bus goes to the zoo (B) how often does the zoo bus run (C) is there a zoo around here (D) is this the right way to the zoo
答案：A — which bus goes to the zoo
文法重點
wh-疑問詞引導的間接問句
完整英文句子
Can you tell me which bus goes to the zoo?
中文翻譯
你可以告訴我哪一班公車開往動物園嗎？
為什麼選這個答案
tell me 後面接的是名詞子句／間接問句，必須使用陳述句語序。which bus 在子句中本身就是主詞，所以直接接 goes。
選項辨析／補充
(B) 應改為 how often the zoo bus runs；(C) 應改為 if/whether there is a zoo around here；(D) 應改為 if/whether this is the right way to the zoo。
文法 3
‘The body clock’ is the mechanism in our body that controls _____ go to sleep.
(A) when should we (B) where should we (C) when we should (D) the time which we should
答案：C — when we should
文法重點
間接問句：wh- + S + V
完整英文句子
‘The body clock’ is the mechanism in our body that controls when we should go to sleep.
中文翻譯
『生理時鐘』是我們體內控制我們何時該入睡的機制。
為什麼選這個答案
controls 後面需要一個名詞子句作受詞。間接問句不用疑問句倒裝，因此應為 when we should go to sleep。
選項辨析／補充
直接問句是 When should we go to sleep?；放入另一個句子後改成 when we should go to sleep。
文法 4
When Felix patted the boy’s head, the boy’s father looked angrily at him. Yet, he didn’t know _____.
(A) what went wrong (B) what did it go wrong (C) why was it wrong (D) how it goes wrong
答案：A — what went wrong
文法重點
名詞子句與 what 作主詞
完整英文句子
When Felix patted the boy’s head, the boy’s father looked angrily at him. Yet, he didn’t know what went wrong.
中文翻譯
Felix 拍了那男孩的頭後，男孩的父親生氣地看著他。然而，他不知道出了什麼問題。
為什麼選這個答案
know 後面接名詞子句。what 在 what went wrong 中作主詞，因此語序本來就是 what + went wrong，不需使用 did。
選項辨析／補充
go wrong 表『出錯；出問題』。間接問句若 wh-詞本身作主詞，不另外加入助動詞 do/does/did。

文法 6
Choose the incorrect sentence.
(A) What you have to do is to take a break. (B) I wonder how does the system work. (C) No one knows what will happen next. (D) Max is not what he used to be.
答案：B — I wonder how does the system work.
文法重點
間接問句不可倒裝
完整英文句子
錯誤句：I wonder how does the system work. → 正確：I wonder how the system works.
中文翻譯
選出錯誤的句子。B：I wonder how does the system work.（錯）
為什麼選這個答案
wonder 後面接間接問句，應用陳述句語序，所以 B 應改成 I wonder how the system works.。
選項辨析／補充
(A) What you have to do 是名詞子句作主詞；(C) what will happen next 中 what 作主詞；(D) what he used to be 表『他以前的樣子／身分』，皆可成立。

(A) amaze (B) amazing (C) amazingly (D) amazed
答案：B — amazing
文法重點
-ing / -ed 形容詞與 one of the most

Dolphins have one of the most amazing sleeping secrets: only half their brain is asleep at one time!
